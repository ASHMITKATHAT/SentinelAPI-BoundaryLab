## Claim discipline

Facts: published feature descriptions, documented protocol/library behavior and contents of the supplied problem statement. Inferences: our narrow workflow may be easier for a particular small team. Hypotheses: willingness to pay, prices, interview gates, setup time, cost estimates and future advantage. Measurements: **none for the scanner yet**.

No defensible source establishes the exact total number of API security platforms, how many of the 250 teams choose each track, a probability of winning, or that no competitor supports our exact workflow. Those claims are intentionally absent.

Only brief paraphrases are used. Refer directly to the linked source for full methodology, terms, limitations and product details. Recheck vendor terms and current releases before committing to an integration or procurement decision.
