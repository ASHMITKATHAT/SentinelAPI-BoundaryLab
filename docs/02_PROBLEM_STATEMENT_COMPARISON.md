# Track comparison and final selection

**Current decision: PS3, chosen explicitly by the team.** Earlier PS1 recommendations are historical and do not govern this build.

| Track | Core problem | Strong demonstration | Principal execution risk | Business hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PS1 Surplus-to-Shelter | Connect surplus food to recipients under timing and transport constraints | A changed operational commitment enables a rescue | Verifying real donor/recipient behavior and real outcomes | Coordination software or sponsored operations; willingness to pay requires validation |
| PS2 CityPulse | Combine civic signals without misleading interpretation | Multiple feeds corroborate a useful incident | Data availability, noisy signals, unverified causality | Campus/city operations buyer; procurement may be slow |
| PS3 SentinelAPI | Find API access-control and related weaknesses with evidence | Permission leak → wrong repair → correct repair on real HTTP traffic | Crowded market, missing policy oracle, unsafe scope | Recurring regression workflow for SaaS engineering teams |

PS3 fits the chosen goal because controlled fixtures permit falsifiable results within two days, and repeat release use creates a plausible subscription motion. The team must still earn differentiation through execution and policy design. A security-specific product is harder to explain than a social-impact story; the demo therefore begins with a familiar sharing-and-download situation.
